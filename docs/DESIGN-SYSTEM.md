# WeddingVerse — Design System Specification

## Visual Philosophy: Editorial Luxury & Warm Modernism
Wedding invitations celebrate one of life's most momentous milestones. The interface blends the quiet sophistication of luxury print typography with the fluidity of modern web motion.

### 1. Typography
- **Display & Headings**: Cormorant Garamond / Instrument Serif (Classic, high-contrast, editorial dignity)
- **Body & Controls**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean, legible, modern geometric sans)
- **Tabular Figures**: `tabular-nums` for dates, countdowns, timestamps, and guest metrics.

### 2. Color Palette (60-30-10 Rule)
- **60% Dominant Neutral Canvas**: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Alabaster / Rice Paper)
- **30% Structural Surfaces**: `#FFFFFF` (Pristine Cards), `#F3EFEA` (Subtle Dividers), `#191614` (Deep Charcoal Ink)
- **10% Accent Budget**: `#B38B45` (Hand-burnished Antique Gold), with deep royal tones (`#6E1727` Royal Crimson, `#1B362A` Emerald, `#1A2536` Royal Sapphire).

### 3. Motion Principles (GSAP & Motion)
- **Hero Reveal**: Orchestrated stagger of royal crest, headline, couple names, and interactive dual-output device mockup.
- **ScrollTrigger Choreography**: Smooth fade-up, parallax depth for decorative botanicals, template horizontal reel, and live interactive feature showcase.
- **Interaction Budgets**: Hover and click interactions complete within $\le 200\text{ms}$ with smooth cubic easing.
